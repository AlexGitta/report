Report GitHub

Report it once. We get it to the right people.

Potholes, fly-tipping, missed bins, graffiti, anti-social behaviour. Take a photo and the app works out what the problem is, whether it’s for the council or the police, and sends it on.

How it works

  1. Snap it

    Add a photo. The app suggests what the problem is and writes a description.

  2. Pin it

    It uses where the photo was taken, or where you are. Adjust it on the map if needed.

  3. We route it

    Council or police? County or district? It works that out for anywhere in England.

  4. Track it

    Follow it from sent to fixed, with updates from neighbours along the way.

What’s inside

  • All 317 councils in England, including two-tier areas, plus all 39 English police forces.
  • 152 published council email addresses across 68 councils, and over 2,500 links to councils’ own reporting forms.
  • Privacy by default. Photos that may show faces or number plates stay private until checked, and anti-social behaviour locations are shown approximately.
  • Built with Expo for iOS, Android and web, Supabase, and Claude for photo recognition.

Council and police data: ONS Open Geography, GOV.UK Local Links and data.police.uk, under the Open Government Licence. Web maps © OpenStreetMap contributors; iPhone screenshots use Apple Maps.
